I am a **Research Associate at the Indian School of Business (ISB)** working at the intersection of **Information Systems, Labor Economics, and Artificial Intelligence**. My research examines how **AI systems reconfigure tasks and skills within organizations**, with a particular focus on how firms operationalize automation, augmentation, and task reallocation in practice.

My work combines **large-scale job-posting data**, **occupation and skill taxonomies**, and **machine learning / large language models** to study changes in skill demand, task composition, and organizational capability building. I am especially interested in how AI adoption alters **who performs which tasks**, how skills are recombined rather than displaced, and what these changes imply for labor markets and public policy.

Currently, I work under **Professor Deepa Mani** on projects supported by the **National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)**, where I help design **indigenous skill taxonomies**, develop **LLM-based skill and task extraction pipelines**, and analyze **high-frequency labor market indicators** for India.

Before joining ISB, I worked for over **2.5 years in industry** in data science and analytics roles. This experience informs my research approach by grounding empirical questions in real organizational and technological constraints.

I intend to apply to **PhD programs in Information Systems / Operations & Technology**, with long-term goals of pursuing a **research-oriented academic career**.

**Research interests:**
- Human–AI collaboration and task reconfiguration  
- AI, skills, and labor markets  
- Job-posting analytics and occupational taxonomies  
- Machine learning and LLMs for social science measurement  
- Technology adoption and organizational capability building
