Namaste :pray: and thank you for visiting my website.

I build high-frequency measurement systems to decode the changing language of work. As an empirical researcher at the Indian School of Business, I work at the intersection of Information Systems and Labor Economics, using Large Language Models (LLMs) and transformer-based pipelines to understand how AI is rewriting the 'DNA' of tasks and skills. My research moves beyond observing technology adoption to exploring the practical frictions it creates: How do firms signal new requirements in an AI-driven market? When does a 'skill mismatch' occur simply because of how work is articulated? And how do these information gaps affect worker mobility and inequality?
If you’re thinking about the future of AI, I’d love to trade notes.
